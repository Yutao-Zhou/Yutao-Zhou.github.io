Optional analytics

With your permission, these projects use Umami to measure visits and feature use. Examples include which reports and reviews are opened, how far they are read, which tool tabs are used, and clicks on sources, downloads, and external links. The tracker receives page paths, referring sites and paths, and basic browser and device information. The analytics configuration removes URL query strings and fragments and excludes form values, mortgage inputs, saved plans, and precise location.

Analytics are off until you choose Allow analytics. Your choice applies to projects on the same site origin and is saved in your browser. Use Analytics preferences in any project’s footer to change it. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track keep analytics off.

Features you choose to use

  • Portfolio and résumé contact forms use Formspree to forward the email address and message you choose to send. Earnings Lens uses FormSubmit to forward stock requests. The hosted FormSubmit page receives the ticker and optional reason when you continue, then asks for your name and email if you finish sending.
  • The weather tool sends the city you search for to OpenWeather. Use my location requests browser permission and sends coordinates to OpenWeather for the requested forecast.
  • Projects may store settings, to-do items or mortgage plans in this browser. Mortgage share links contain the calculation inputs that you choose to share.
  • GitHub Pages and services that deliver requested fonts, map tiles, charts, or assets can receive connection information such as your IP address when serving content. Links to other sites are subject to those sites’ practices.

隐私与数据使用

经你同意后，Umami 才会统计页面访问和功能使用情况，例如打开了哪些报告或车评、阅读进度、使用了哪些工具标签，以及来源、下载和外部链接的点击。统计会收到页面路径、来源站点及路径和基本浏览器或设备信息。统计排除网址查询参数和片段、表单内容、房贷输入、保存方案和精确定位。

选择「允许统计」后才开启访问统计。选择保存在当前浏览器，适用于同一网站来源下的项目。你可以从任意项目的页脚随时更改或撤回。浏览器的 Global Privacy Control 或 Do Not Track 信号会关闭统计。更改统计设置。

个人网站和履历的联系表单通过 Formspree 转交你主动发送的邮箱地址和留言。Earnings Lens 的股票申请使用 FormSubmit，点击继续时会将股票和选填理由带到 FormSubmit，完成发送时由它收集姓名和邮箱。天气搜索会将你查询的城市发送给 OpenWeather；选择使用当前位置并授权后，会发送坐标查询天气。

部分项目在当前浏览器保存设置、待办事项或房贷方案。你主动分享的房贷计算链接包含计算输入。GitHub Pages 和提供字体、地图、图表或其他资源的服务，在提供内容时可能收到 IP 地址等连接信息。外部链接遵循目标站点的做法。

如对本说明或提交的数据有疑问，请使用个人网站的联系表单。
